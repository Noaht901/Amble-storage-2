FAQ

Answers to the questions we hear most

Can I access my unit 24/7?

Yes. All customers get secure 24-hour access, every day of the year. You come and go on your schedule.

How secure is the facility?

The site is monitored by CCTV around the clock, with a secure perimeter and individually locked units. You’re the only one with a key.

How long do I need to commit for?

There’s no long-term contract. Store for as little as a week, or as long as you need — just give simple notice when you’re ready to move out.

What if the size I want isn’t available?

Pop your details on our follow-up list. We’ll call you the moment a matching unit frees up — often within a couple of weeks.

How quickly can I move in?

Often the same day. The paperwork is short and professional, and our team will walk you through everything on the spot.

Can businesses receive deliveries to their unit?

Yes — many of our business customers use us as a stockroom. Chat to us about delivery arrangements and we’ll make it work.
